# The Faithfulness of God

## Introduction

Good morning, church. It is good to be together again. Today we are looking at a promise that has carried believers through every generation, through good seasons and hard ones.

[Pause. Look up at the camera.]

Before we begin, let me ask you a question. When was the last time you felt that God had forgotten you? Hold that moment in your mind as we read together.

## The promise

> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.
> John chapter 3, verse 16

Notice the first word of the promise. It does not begin with us. It begins with God. Before we ever loved him, he loved us.

Pastor Kim Jae-won [JEH-won] once told me a story about this verse. When he was a young man in Seoul, he worked nights at a small print shop. Every night he would set this verse in type, letter by letter, for a church bulletin he could not afford to keep.

He said, God is faithful. Even when I am not, God is faithful.

## The promise is for you

In Lamentations chapter 3, verses 22 and 23, we read that his mercies are new every morning. Great is your faithfulness.

[Slower here.]

That means this morning's mercy was made for this morning. It was not left over from yesterday, and it will not run out before tomorrow.

So if you came here today feeling forgotten, hear this. God is faithful. Even when we are not, God is faithful.

## Closing prayer

Let us pray. Father, thank you for your faithfulness to us, for your mercy that is new every morning, and for the love you showed us in your Son. Amen.
